# SlushTec Brand Pack

**Locked 2026-08-12**  
Studio identity for SlushTec (umbrella for systems-driven game projects, permanent residual marks, Sonoran iron aesthetic).

Brand Promise: **You act. The world keeps score.**

---

## 1. Core Marks

### Primary Wordmark
- All-caps **SLUSHTEC**
- Angular geometric sans with metallic texture
- Proportional emphasis on **S** and **T** (stronger visual weight and geometric cuts)
- Color split: Iron Black (#0f0e0d) on “SLUSH” → Golden Glow (#e8bd79) on “TEC”
- Thin broken golden underline (score residual)
- Use for headers, splash, Steam, marketing

### Primary Mark (Circular S)
- Bold angular geometric **S** inside double-ring circular seal
- Dark iron S + Golden Glow outer ring
- Strong silhouette, wearable badge energy
- Primary for favicon, social avatar, app icon, merch, laser engraving

### Lockups
- Horizontal: Circular S + Wordmark (preferred for nav / headers)
- Stacked (for tall formats)
- Mark alone (small sizes ≤ 64 px)

---

## 2. Color Tokens

| Token            | Hex       | Usage                              |
|------------------|-----------|------------------------------------|
| Iron Black       | `#0f0e0d` | Primary background, wordmark body  |
| Golden Glow      | `#e8bd79` | Accents, TEC, underline, rings     |
| Warm Earth       | `#de865b` | Secondary accent, hover, CTAs      |
| Burnt Adobe      | `#904d2b` | Deep accent, borders               |
| Sage             | `#659176` | Success / positive residual        |
| Pure Black       | `#000000` | Deepest field                      |
| Off-White        | `#f5f2eb` | Text on dark, reverse              |

**Rule:** High contrast only. Never place Golden Glow on light backgrounds without Iron Black container.

---

## 3. Typography

- **Display / Logo:** Custom angular geometric (match wordmark cuts). No soft curves.
- **Headings:** Bold geometric sans (Inter Tight, Space Grotesk, or similar).
- **Body:** Clean high-legibility sans (Inter, system-ui). High contrast.
- Avoid rounded or playful typefaces.

---

## 4. Clear Space & Minimum Sizes

- Clear space around any mark = height of the capital S.
- Minimum digital size: 32 px for Circular S, 120 px wide for wordmark.
- Laser engraving: convert to pure black/white vector. Minimum line weight equivalent to 1.5 pt at final size.

---

## 5. Usage Rules

**Do**
- Use Circular S as the residual badge (merch, favicon, Discord, Steam avatar).
- Keep the broken underline on the wordmark — it is the score line.
- Prefer mono versions for laser, embroidery, single-color print.
- Maintain proportional emphasis on S and T.

**Don’t**
- Stretch, rotate, or add effects that break the geometric cuts.
- Place the full color wordmark on busy or low-contrast backgrounds.
- Soften the angles or introduce gradients inside the letterforms.
- Use the marks for non-SlushTec projects without approval.

---

## 6. Laser & Merch

Primary use case: Sonoran Slush laser engraving.
- Supply pure black (filled or outline) versions.
- Circular S is the preferred mark for coins, coasters, tumblers, crystal.
- Wordmark works well as a horizontal engrave on larger items.

---

## 7. Target Audience Signal

The visual system is tuned for players who value:
- Competence over spectacle
- Permanent consequence
- Systems that keep score
- Residual marks that feel earned

Tone: industrial desert iron, permanent, high-trust, no soft systems.
